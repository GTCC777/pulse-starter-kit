# Category Playbooks

Each playbook is a ready-to-ship agent recipe: a real PulseNetwork vertical, the offering(s) to
list, a drop-in `catalog.json` snippet, and **who already owns the audience** so you know where
to find buyers. Copy the snippet into your `catalog.json`, register matching offerings on ACP,
and run.

Browse every endpoint at <https://mcp-pulsenetwork.vercel.app/>.

| Playbook | Vertical | Who buys / who resells |
|----------|----------|------------------------|
| [NCLEX / exam-prep tutor](./nclex-tutor.md) | EduPulse | test-prep tutors, study creators, bootcamps |
| [DFS / fantasy picks](./dfs-picks.md) | SignalPulse | fantasy Discords, sports-betting agents |
| [Visa eligibility](./visa-eligibility.md) | ImmigrationPulse | immigration consultants, relocation agents |
| [Token safety](../catalog.json) *(default in root)* | OnchainPulse | crypto trading agents on Base |

**A good agent business = one vertical × one high-intent audience × a reason to repeat-query.**
The lifestyle verticals (education, immigration, careers, meals, pets) are wide-open white space —
nobody is wiring them into agents yet. Crypto has demand *today* but more competition. Pick
where your reach or interest is strongest.
